Word of the Day 🌟
A fun, interactive, kid-friendly app designed to help young learners build their vocabulary, understand word meanings, and confidently use new words in their everyday lives!

🚀 Overview
Building a strong vocabulary early in life improves reading comprehension, boosts communication skills, and fosters confidence. Word of the Day makes learning new words an exciting daily adventure rather than a chore.

Instead of dry dictionary definitions, the app teaches words using engaging visuals, kid-friendly explanations, audio pronunciations, and real-world examples that children can relate to at home, school, or play.

✨ Key Features
📅 One New Word Every Day: Carefully curated, age-appropriate words designed to stretch vocabulary without feeling overwhelming.

🗣️ Read & Listen: Native audio pronunciations so kids learn how to say words correctly.

💡 Kid-Friendly Definitions: Explanations written in plain, simple language that young minds can easily digest.

🏫 Real-World Context: "Try using it today!" section gives kids specific examples of how to use the word with family and friends.

🎨 Interactive Visuals: Illustrations and fun animations to reinforce visual learning and memory retention.

⭐ Favorite Words & Badges: Kids can save their favorite words and earn achievement badges for maintaining daily learning streaks.

🎮 Quick Mini-Games: Short, optional daily quizzes and matching games to reinforce what they've learned.
